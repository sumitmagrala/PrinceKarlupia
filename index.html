Punjabi Traditions

The joyous cultural customs that make our celebration unforgettable

Sehra Bandi

Groom's Blessing & Crown

The auspicious tying of the royal floral and pearl Sehra around the groom's turban by his sisters and family elders, blessing him with dignity, pride, and grace.

🐎

Baraat & Milni

Royal Procession & Embrace

The groom arrives in a grand royal procession with dhol and celebration, met at the entrance by the warm ceremonial Milni garland embrace uniting corresponding family heads.

The blissful sacred union circumambulating the Guru Granth Sahib Ji through four hymns of spiritual love, binding the bride and groom as one soul in two bodies.
